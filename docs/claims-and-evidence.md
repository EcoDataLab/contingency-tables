## aggregate-actual-gap

The [actual aggregate trace theorem](aggregate-defect-trace.md) proves
`Var≤34848d⁵s³ E`, with `s=m+n−2`, for the same canonical rational trace
as the earlier d⁸ theorem. Its squared aspect statement is
`Var²≤34848²A³d¹³E²` under `A≥0,s²≤Ad`. The fractional-power interpretation
gives `34848 A^(3/2)d^(13/2)` and uses nonnegative variance and energy.

The original-margin donor budget, full completed-table repair injection,
actual correction cap, full variance and trace normalization are derived in
the [additive formal packet](../research/aggregate-defect-trace/). The trace
headlines retain `m,n≥2`, `p≥2`, and nonempty positive defect support;
both completion branches retain `Q=4p−3`. The sharper reviewed `15488`
coefficient below is distinct. Relative to the earlier shape coefficient
`3872d⁶s²`, the improvement ratio is `d/(9s)`; the smaller bound should be used.
No complete sampler runtime follows from this inverse-gap result.

## complete-bounded-sampler

`Math115.LatticeBoundedSampling.boundedSampling` proves the original `Algorithms.BoundedSamplingStatement`: one fixed fair-bit machine and global natural constants `C>0,D` work for every finite `m,n`, natural margins `r:Fin m→ℕ,c:Fin n→ℕ` with equal totals, and numeric precision `h≥1`. Zero margins and empty dimensions are included. With `M=∑ᵢrᵢ`, `S=m+n+clog₂(M+1)+h+1`, and `t=C*S^D`, every `t`-bit tape halts with a feasible ordinary table. Under uniform independent fair bits, the output law at that same time is within total variation `2^-h` of uniform ordinary fixed-margin tables.

Pinned upstream `OAI.ContingencyTables.boundedSampling` already proves exactly this same type. This is an alternative proof through a different fixed machine and the d¹⁷ route, not a stronger existential bounded-sampling statement. The [reserved-bit comparison](composed-complexity.md#comparable-result-in-the-same-public-size-measure) gives a concrete common quantity whose upper allowance improves.

The final statement is existential; its proof chooses the fixed [public machine](../formal/Math115/PublicLatticeSampler.lean). `public_spec` identifies its encoded table draw, `machine_outputs` establishes every-tape halting and feasibility, `machine_mass` identifies its output probabilities, and `machineTime_public_bound` supplies the uniform time bound. The [complete checkpoint](outer-sampler-compiler-checkpoint.md) retains the exact statement map and evidence. The separate [explicit runtime-degree claim](#explicit-runtime-degree) now fixes the degree for that same machine; measured performance remains unknown. Neither `d¹⁷` nor the reserved-bit/input bound `S¹⁷⁰` is the machine-time degree. Strict Comparator acceptance of this sampler, an exact-sampling extension, and counting are separate from this bounded near-uniform result.

separate environment audit using the pinned Lean 4.34.1 APIs. A driver imports
the target compiled modules and the helper, then runs:

```lean
#environment_audit prefixes [Math115] modules [] headlines
  [Math115.IdealRepairRefinement.feasibleChain_poincare_d17_refined]
```

The helper reads `Environment.constants`, selects declarations by namespace
or defining-module prefix, or by an explicitly selected imported module, and emits their exact pretty-printed
Lean types, declaration kinds, direct dependencies, and transitive axiom

selected environment, including declarations that were never placed in a
manual `#print axioms` roster. Defining-module selection also includes private
names; helper instrumentation is excluded from the project inventory.

opaque/theorem values and inductive constructors. Standard axioms are reported
separately from nonstandard axioms; any nonstandard axiom fails the command.
The proof-statement records contain all binders and hypotheses, while the
ledger supplies the separate English interpretation.
